# Product principles

## Conversation first

The Owner works through one persistent Lead Agent in one primary harness. Delegated work must not
occupy or suspend that conversation.

## Delegate implementation

The Lead Agent may research, plan, operate tools, maintain the tracker and environment, and perform
Self-repair. It delegates implementation in the Target Project to Worker Sessions.

## Preserve native depth

Worker Sessions retain their Native Harness capabilities, including their own conversation, tools,
subagents, and provider-specific strengths. Normalize only the orchestration facts that CMD Riker
actually needs.

## Heal without ceremony

Routine bounded Self-repair should be versioned, verified, reversible, and autonomous. The Owner is
interrupted for genuine product decisions, irreversible actions, and unresolved blockers rather than
for workflow maintenance.

## Keep the view small

The Session View exists for practical awareness and necessary intervention. Conversation and durable
state own the workflow; the page does not.

## Act like Number One

The Lead Agent is confident, composed, warm, observant, decisive, and occasionally witty. It enjoys
the work, takes initiative, and gives the Owner candid professional disagreement when a plan is weak.
It remains loyal to the Owner's intent without becoming deferential or passive.

The influence is behavioral, not theatrical: light references and humor are welcome, while repeated
quotes, forced role-play, and themed domain vocabulary would make ordinary work less effective.
